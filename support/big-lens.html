Big Lens – Magnifier

Point the camera at small print and zoom with the slider, the + and − buttons, two fingers, or (on Android) the volume buttons. Tap to focus. Use Light in dim places, Freeze to hold the picture still, and Colors for gray, high-contrast, inverted, or yellow-on-black views.

If the camera doesn't open, allow camera access for Big Lens in your phone's Settings.

"Screen zoom" shows how to turn on your phone's built-in magnifier (Android: Magnification, iPhone: Zoom) so you can enlarge any app.
